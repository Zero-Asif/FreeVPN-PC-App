The FreeProxy VPN extension is now in this browser

It was installed by the FreeProxy VPN desktop app -- nothing else added it.

NEW
Checking the desktop app... Asking the app what it is doing right now.

What it does

  • Sends this browser's traffic through the Tor engine inside the desktop app while you are connected, using the same split-tunnel exceptions you set in the app.
  • Reports the connected country's location to websites that ask for your position, instead of your real one. Chromium normally works your location out from the Wi-Fi networks around you, which a proxy cannot change -- so this part has to be done in the browser.
  • Gives you the whole app in the toolbar: every country, connect, disconnect, the kill switch and the split-tunnel list, in sync with the app window in both directions.

Three things worth knowing

  1. It only works while the desktop app is running. The extension has no VPN of its own -- it points this browser at the app's engine. With the app closed it goes inert and hands the browser straight back to your normal connection.
  2. Pin it, so it is one click away. Open the extensions button in the toolbar and use the pin next to FreeProxy VPN Extension.
  3. You do not have to use it. The desktop app already routes the whole PC. This extension is what makes the location a website reads match the country you picked, and what lets you drive the app without leaving the browser.
▲ This icon, up in the toolbar, is the extension. Click it any time to connect, switch country or turn the kill switch on.

Removing the desktop app removes this extension with it, along with every proxy, DNS, IPv6 and location setting it changed. Nothing is left behind.
