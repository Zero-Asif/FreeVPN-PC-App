# Privacy Policy for FreeProxy VPN

**Effective Date:** April 22, 2026

Welcome to FreeProxy VPN ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your online anonymity. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices when you use the FreeProxy VPN desktop application (the "Software").

## 1. Zero-Logs Policy
FreeProxy VPN operates strictly under a **Zero-Logs Policy**. We do not track, collect, store, or share any of your personal information, browsing history, connection timestamps, session durations, bandwidth usage, or IP addresses.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect
Because our software is built to provide maximum privacy using the Tor network, we explicitly **do not** collect:
* **Personal Identification Information:** (Names, emails, phone numbers).
* **Financial Data:** (The software is completely free and open-source).
* **Device Identifiers or Telemetry:** We do not track hardware IDs or usage statistics.
* **DNS Queries or Web Traffic:** We do not monitor or log what websites you visit.

## 3. How the Software Works (Local Processing)
The FreeProxy VPN client runs entirely on your local Windows machine. 
* **Administrator Permissions:** The application requires Windows Administrator privileges exclusively to securely modify your local system's proxy settings and enforce DNS/WebRTC leak protection. 
* **No Cloud Servers:** We do not maintain any centralized cloud servers to route your data. All network routing is handled locally by the integrated Tor engine.
* **No Analytics:** We do not embed any third-party analytics, crash reporting, or tracking scripts within the Software.

## 4. Third-Party Networks (The Tor Network)
FreeProxy VPN utilizes the open-source Tor Network to securely route your traffic. 
* Your internet traffic is encrypted and bounced through multiple independent, volunteer-operated nodes globally.
* We do not own, operate, or control these Tor nodes.
* While we provide the interface to connect to this network, the inherent design of Tor ensures your anonymity.

## 5. Open Source Transparency
FreeProxy VPN is completely open-source. Anyone can review, audit, and compile the source code directly from our official GitHub repository. We believe that true privacy relies on absolute transparency.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this document with an updated "Effective Date." Since we do not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes. Please review this policy periodically on our GitHub repository.
