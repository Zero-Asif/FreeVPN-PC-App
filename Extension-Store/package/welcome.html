The FreeProxy VPN extension is now in this browser

It works with the FreeProxy VPN desktop app on this PC. Here is what it does, and what it needs.

NEW
Checking the desktop app... Asking the app what it is doing right now.

What it does

  • Sets this browser's proxy to the Tor engine inside the desktop app while you are connected, using the same split-tunnel exceptions you set in the app. It is handed back the moment you disconnect, close the app, or remove this extension.
  • Reports the connected country's location to websites that ask for your position, instead of your real one. Microsoft Edge normally works your location out from the Wi-Fi networks around you, which a proxy cannot change -- so this part has to be done in the browser.
  • Clears this browser's cache, cookies and history when you switch to a different country, along with the stored site data of the sites that were given the country you are leaving. Without it those sites keep reporting the old country -- Google, for example, keeps the location in a cookie. Because the cookies go too, you are signed out of sites you were signed in to: that is the price of being certain nothing is still holding the country you left. It happens only when you change country, never on an ordinary disconnect and never on its own.
  • Gives you the whole app in the toolbar: every country, connect, disconnect, the kill switch and the split-tunnel list, in sync with the app window in both directions.

Three things worth knowing

  1. It only works while the FreeProxy VPN desktop app is running. The extension has no VPN of its own -- it points this browser at the app's engine. The app is free, open source and Windows 10/11 only; if it is not on this PC yet, get it from github.com/Zero-Asif/FreeVPN-PC-App. With the app closed this extension goes inert and hands the browser straight back to your normal connection.
  2. Pin it, so it is one click away. Open the extensions button in the toolbar and use the pin next to FreeProxy VPN Extension.
  3. You do not have to use it. The desktop app already routes the whole PC. This extension is what makes the location a website reads match the country you picked, and what lets you drive the app without leaving the browser.
▲ This icon, up in the toolbar, is the extension. Click it any time to connect, switch country or turn the kill switch on.

Nothing here is collected, logged or sent anywhere: the only address this extension ever talks to is the FreeProxy VPN app on your own PC, over 127.0.0.1. No account, no login, no ads, no analytics -- read the privacy policy. You can switch this extension off or remove it at any time from edge://extensions; it is never installed in a way that takes that switch away from you. Removing the desktop app releases every proxy, DNS, IPv6 and location setting the app changed.
